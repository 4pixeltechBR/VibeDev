# VibeDev is not an issue tracker

VibeDev's `decision_log` and `backlog` are **personal governance artifacts**, not team coordination tools.

## What VibeDev tracks
- Decisions YOU made in this project
- Ideas YOU deferred to later
- Your kill criteria and progress

## What VibeDev does NOT do
- Multi-user assignment
- Issue status workflows (open/in-progress/done)
- Comments, threads, mentions
- Cross-project queries
- Team notifications

## For team coordination, use
- **GitHub Issues / GitLab Issues** for open source
- **Linear** for product teams
- **Jira** for enterprise
- **Backlog.md** for local files in-repo

VibeDev can **produce** an issue list (after `/vd-plan` runs), but it does not **manage** issues.

## Why this is out of scope

Issue tracking is a mature category with dedicated tools. Building it into VibeDev would:
- Compete poorly with Linear/Jira/etc.
- Distract from VibeDev's core: helping one person ship one project
- Require backend, real-time updates, permission models — engineering 10x VibeDev's current scope
